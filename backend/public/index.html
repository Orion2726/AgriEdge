Registered phones

Issue an Official Advisory

Reaches every connected phone within about 15 seconds, then passes phone to phone over Bluetooth to phones without internet. Shown in the app with an “Official” mark.

Official Advisories

“Withdraw” stops phones from fetching an advisory. Phones that already carry it keep it until it expires.

Field Reports from Farmers

Reports uploaded by phones. via mesh means another phone carried the report here over Bluetooth. A signature proves which phone made a report, not that the diagnosis is correct.

Mandi Prices ₹ per quintal

Live Agmarknet prices, fetched every 3 hours (see Data Sources). Editing a price makes it manual — it is kept until you press “Use live”. Online phones sync changes; offline phones receive them over Bluetooth.

Minimum Support Price ₹ per quintal

Farm Supplies Marketplace listings posted by farmers from the app

Farmers list produce, seed or tools in the app’s Sell tab; others buy them in the Buy tab. Buying needs a live connection to this server, which marks the item sold so it can’t be sold twice. Each sale also appears under Orders.

Orders

Store orders and marketplace sales from phones. A status change shows in the app within about 15 seconds.

Soil Test Reports

Soil Health Card values submitted from phones.

Connect a Phone

  1. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
  2. In the app open More → Sync & server.
  3. Enter the address above and tap Save & test.
  4. Phones without internet get the data over Bluetooth from a connected phone.
